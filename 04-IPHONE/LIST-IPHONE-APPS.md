# List every app on your iPhone

Shortcuts cannot output a list of installed apps ([r/shortcuts](https://www.reddit.com/r/shortcuts/comments/10ij3y7/shortcut_for_listing_all_apps_installed/)). Use one of these:

1. Settings > General > iPhone Storage: every installed app with size and last used ([Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108429)).
2. Settings > Apps (iOS 18 and later): all apps alphabetically ([Apple Community](https://discussions.apple.com/thread/255824486)).
3. Home Screen: swipe to the App Library, tap the search bar, scroll the A-Z list.
4. App Store > your profile > Purchased: everything you ever downloaded, including apps no longer installed.
5. Best for an export: take screenshots of lists 1 or 2 and send them here. I convert them to a Markdown table in the vault, grouped by purpose.
6. From a PC with USB: `ideviceinstaller list` prints installed apps ([libimobiledevice man page](https://github.com/libimobiledevice/ideviceinstaller/blob/master/man/ideviceinstaller.1)). Run it over remote desktop only if the phone is plugged into that PC.
